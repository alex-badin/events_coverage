Public attention against media coverage

The survey share is how often people named the event unprompted; the post count is how many posts matched it. How many events are loaded is in the tiles below.

Each event by survey share and by number of matched posts

The two axes are different kinds of measurement — a share of survey answers and a count of posts — so read where an event sits on each one separately. The distance between the axes means nothing. An event marked ⚠ was matched on different settings, so its post count is not on the same footing as the others; the exact settings are shown when you select it.
