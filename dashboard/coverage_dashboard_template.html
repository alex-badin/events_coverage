What Russians noticed, and how their media covered it

Every week the FOM survey asks people which events of the past days they remember. This page follows those events over five years, grouped into themes, and shows how six groups of Russian-language media covered the ones measured so far.

What people noticed, week by week

One row per area of news. Click a row name to open its themes; click a week to see that week's list.

Coverage measured so far

For each event with calculated coverage: how much of its own output each media group gave it. The respondent percentage comes from the FOM survey, not from media posts.

Per 1,000 = matched posts per 1,000 posts the group published in the event's search window (3 days before the survey week to 10 days after). The colour compares each group with the median of the groups for the same event, so it shows which groups gave the event more or less room than the others; it does not compare one event with another. Faded and outlined: fewer than 5 posts, too few to compare, and left out of the median.
